How to play

  1. One player from the team takes the phone and looks at the card.
  2. Press Start round and get your team to guess as many cards as you can before time runs out. Act, explain, make sounds: any way works.
  3. The team must say the exact word, phrasal verb or idiom in English.
  4. When time is up, pass the phone to the other team. The card above is an example.
  5. Need a break? Pause freezes the timer and hides the card. Stop round ends the round early and keeps the points.

Points

Skips cost nothing, but the card goes to the bottom of the deck. Keys: Space start · → or Enter guessed · ← skip · P pause.

Spy

Hmm, who is who?

How to play Spy

  1. Get your card. Pass the phone around. Each player taps to see their card, then hides it.
  2. Give clues. Take turns saying one short sentence about your card. Don't say the card itself!
  3. Vote. Talk, ask questions, then choose who to kick out.
  4. Win. Players win when every spy is out. Spies win if they last until there are as many spies as other players.

Spy knows: the spy sees "You are the spy" and no card. If caught, the spy can still win by guessing the card. Secret spy: the spy gets a similar card and doesn't know they are the spy.
